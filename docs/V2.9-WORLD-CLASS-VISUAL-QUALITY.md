# V2.9 World-Class Visual Quality

V2.9 separates provider success, technical validity, visual quality, semantic/creative compliance, temporal quality, audio/editorial quality, final-master quality, and human approval. A decodable MP4 can therefore be `SOURCE_TECHNICAL: PASS` and `SOURCE_VISUAL: FAIL` without receiving a generic production-quality pass.

## Quality tiers and paid boundary

| Tier | Operator meaning | Semantic policy | Representative samples |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `ECONOMY / DRAFT` | Ideation, previews, internal exploration | May continue with a visible WARN if semantic QA is unavailable | 2%, 10%, 30%, 50%, 70%, 90%, 98% |
| `STANDARD` | Commercial/social production baseline and the QUALITY default | Required; preflight blocks before generation when unavailable | 2%, 10%, 30%, 50%, 70%, 90%, 98% |
| `PREMIUM` | Hero and brand-defining production | Required, with stricter thresholds | 1%, 5%, 15%, 30%, 50%, 70%, 85%, 95%, 99% |

Wan STANDARD resolves to the existing 720p, 24 fps, `goFast=false`, prompt-optimized, interpolated profile with 121 frames where compatible. Unsupported profiles fail explicitly; there is no profile downgrade. Preflight exposes generation, speech, and visual-evaluation execution classes and expected evaluator call count. V2.9 never initiates an evaluator or regeneration call silently.

## Pipeline and boundaries

```text
Planning
  -> Pre-Execution Validation
  -> Provider Generation
  -> Source Technical Validation
  -> Source Visual Validation
  -> Temporal Quality
  -> Creative Compliance
  -> Master Assembly
  -> Master Technical Validation
  -> Final Quality
  -> Human Review
```

Each generated source is evaluated immediately after durable provider resolution and before master assembly. A source `FAIL` preserves the failed source and immutable evidence, blocks assembly and review, and reports `paidRegenerationTriggered: false`. A failed rendered master is also preserved as a new immutable version. Human approval remains mandatory and publication remains disabled.

## Evaluator architecture

`VisualQualityEvaluator` combines two replaceable layers:

- Deterministic analysis decodes reproducible timestamp samples, records hashes and image statistics, and checks source resolution, orientation, black/blank output, persistent dividers/panel layouts, outer mattes, and excessive static content.
- `SemanticVisualEvaluatorAdapter` is a provider-neutral interface for future OpenAI, Gemini, local, or other vision evaluators. It receives the sampled evidence, canonical plan, negative intent, tier, provider/model, and resolved settings, and returns structured checks with reasons, confidence, and evidence. The disabled adapter emits `SEMANTIC_VISUAL_QA_NOT_CONFIGURED`; it never invents a pass.

The score model is `WEAKEST_LINK`, not an average. Every check has a stable status and reason. Hard failures such as prohibited triptych/contact-sheet composition, strict no-text artifacts, severe subject/anatomy failure, corrupt/blank/black output, wrong orientation, missing required audio, or voiceover cutoff override aggregate quality.

Deterministic divider detection is deliberately only a strong structural signal. Picture-in-picture, pseudo-text, human integrity, realism, creative intent, identity drift, and cross-shot continuity remain semantic judgments. Cross-shot continuity has its own structured adapter result; multi-shot STANDARD/PREMIUM work fails closed when that implementation is absent, while a single shot records `CONTINUITY_NOT_APPLICABLE`.

## Canonical negative intent and provenance

The creative planner emits structured, provider-neutral negative intent covering one coherent full-frame view, prohibited panel/collage/PiP/UI structures, prohibited generated typography/logos/watermarks, and severe subject duplication/anatomy defects. Provider adapters translate that intent at the media-request boundary for Replicate/Wan, fal.ai/Seedance, Runway, Google/Veo, and Luma.

Important marketing copy, captions, CTA typography, and logos are designated for deterministic post-production rather than model rendering. Provenance retains the canonical prompt, adapter-translated prompt, structured negative intent, provider/model, seed, source probe, and resolved settings.

## Evidence, observability, and learning signals

Sampled JPEG evidence is stored as new immutable artifact versions. Quality JSON references those versions and records checks, timestamps, ratios, hashes, profile, source settings, provider/model, known provider usage/cost/latency when reported, and quality lifecycle. Existing additive JSONB and artifact provenance fields are reused, so V2.9 requires no database migration.

The Dashboard exposes ten independent stages and a shot inspector with source identity, provider/model/profile, source fidelity, prompt/settings/seed, quality results, reason evidence, sample hashes, an explicit low-resolution upscale warning, and a human-controlled `REGENERATE THIS SHOT` action. Structured human rejection categories are retained in review decision metadata for future benchmark analysis; they do not trigger training or automatic routing.

## Audio and editorial scope

V2.9 implements required-audio presence, speech/master duration mismatch, hard-fail voiceover cutoff, contiguous timeline/dead-time, duplicate generated asset, planned hook, and planned CTA checks. Speech intelligibility, clipping, loudness, severe distortion, music masking, semantic continuity, detailed motion defects, and anatomy/text understanding require future configured evaluators; the system reports this limitation as WARN or fail-closed policy rather than awarding a synthetic pass.
