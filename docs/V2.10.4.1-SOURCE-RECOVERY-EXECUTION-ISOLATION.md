# V2.10.4.1 source recovery execution isolation

V2.10.4.1 separates the durable canonical shot revision from the bounded paid execution projection used by `SOURCE_CREATIVE` and `SOURCE_GEOMETRY` recovery.

The canonical revision remains complete and immutable. It retains the complete script, storyboard, asset plan, voice configuration, continuity plan, and revision lineage. The recovery record continues to store that full canonical raw revision.

The execution projection is a deterministic, fingerprinted view containing only:

- the selected replacement video asset;
- its selected shot and a structurally valid script scene;
- voice generation disabled;
- captions and post-production end-title transforms disabled;
- recovery kind, source asset, replacement asset, retry reason, revision number, and canonical revision fingerprint.

Before preflight can become ready, and again after the durable execution is claimed, the prepared runtime plan must prove:

- one video generation;
- zero audio generations;
- one paid media call;
- one semantic evaluation;
- zero continuity evaluations;
- zero renderer jobs;
- two maximum external calls, including evaluator retry policy.

The preflight ID is the execution-projection fingerprint. Confirmation rebuilds the projection from current durable state and rejects a changed instruction, provider/model, replacement identity, revision, or source identity with `PREFLIGHT_STALE`. The claimed execution also re-prepares and verifies the same fingerprint before any provider boundary.

No database migration is required. The V2.10.4 one-attempt recovery constraint and immutable artifact lineage are unchanged.
