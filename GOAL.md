# GOAL

## Product goal
A multi-brand, provider-neutral Content Factory that reliably creates high-quality advertising videos from approved creative inputs through a controlled, auditable production pipeline.

## User outcome
An operator can configure a brand and production, approve script/storyboard, run preflight, explicitly start generation, inspect the final MP4, and approve or reject it without editing code.

## Current milestone
Complete one controlled end-to-end QUALITY production from READY preflight to verified playable MP4 master, with no automatic publication.

## Success proof
Durable production and artifact records; verified MP4 video/audio; relevant technical and visual QA results; operator review; documented provider costs and any failures.
